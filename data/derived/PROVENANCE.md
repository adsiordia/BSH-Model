# Derived data provenance

## reactants_curated.csv
Generated by `src/curate_reactants.py` from `data/raw/bsh_reactants_SMILES_corrected.xlsx`
(sheet `Reactants`, 55 rows: 36 amines + 19 bile acids). The raw file is never modified.

### Correction applied
**2,3-Diaminopropionic acid** — the stored SMILES `O=C(O)[C@@]1(N)NCCC1N` is a cyclic
C5H11N3O2 (MW 145.16), not diaminopropionic acid. The spreadsheet's own MW column reads
104.110, which matches the intended linear C3H8N2O2. Corrected to `NC(CN)C(=O)O`
(MW 104.11, exact match).

Stereochemistry is deliberately not asserted: unlike the other amino acids in the sheet
(`L-Alanine`, `L-Serine`, …) this entry carries no L-/D- prefix, so the corrected SMILES
is achiral.

Consequence: the compound has two nucleophilic nitrogens (alpha- and beta-NH2), so it is
classified `polyamine`, not `amino_acid`. Any descriptor or fingerprint computed from the
raw SMILES was computed on the wrong molecule. It appears in the activity data as
`2,3_diaminopropionic acid`.

### Noted, not changed
**TLCA** — spreadsheet MW (505.7) is the sodium salt; the SMILES is the free acid (483.7),
difference 21.98 = Na - H. The structure is correct, so the SMILES stands.

**HCl salts** — `3-methoxytyramine HCl`, `Dopamine HCl`, `L-Ornithine monohydrochloride`
list the salt MW. The curation desalts the SMILES (keeps the largest fragment), so
`mw_smiles` is the free base and sits ~36.46 below `mw_file`. Expected, not an error.

### Verification performed
- All 55 SMILES parse.
- 51/55 MW agree with the spreadsheet within 1 Da; the 4 gaps are the salt forms above.
- All 19 bile acids carry an intact steroid nucleus, and their OH/keto counts and
  Gly/Tau conjugate match their names (`conjugate_matches_name` is True for all 19).

### Conventions
- `degree` (Mono/Di/Tri) counts **hydroxyls only**; keto groups are excluded.
  So `3a,7a,12k` is Di (2 OH) and `3a,12k` is Mono (1 OH).
- `core` is the identity after deconjugation, so the G- and T- forms of a bile acid
  collapse to one core.
- `n_conjugate_forms` is how many conjugates (Gly, Tau) that core was supplied as.
  Cores supplied as both are at ~2x the core-equivalent concentration of those supplied
  as one, since the protocol used 1 mM of each bile acid.

## bsh_aligned.fasta, conservation_scores.csv, enzyme_amine_activity.csv
Carried over from the v1 repo (`BSH-Model/outputs/`), produced by its notebooks
(MUSCLE alignment; conservation scoring; `notebooks/amine_activity_analysis.ipynb`).

Activity label definitions in `enzyme_amine_activity.csv`, both verified against the data:
- `active_approach1` = `Intensity > threshold`, where threshold is the **maximum intensity
  observed across the 7 controls for that amine**. 11.2% of rows active.
- `active_approach2` = `Intensity > 1000` (a flat hard-coded constant), with taurine and
  glycine forced False. Controls are not used. 21.0% of rows active.

Caution: 50.2% of `active_approach2` positives fall **below** their own amine's control
maximum, concentrated in the high-background amines (gaba: 481 positives, 34 of which
clear the control threshold). Replicates are already collapsed in this file — the source
`ipsita_heatmap_long.csv` holds one intensity per enzyme-product. Replicate-level data
still exists in `data/raw/Trimmed_remove_proteomics.csv` (rep1/rep2/rep3).
